Sempan

A deck-building roguelike where you defeat monsters with arithmetic.

(7+3)×8= 80
Play in your browser Download (Android · iOS · Web)

Beta 2 · The game's interface is served in Korean · Progress is saved on your device

1

Build an equation

Slide your finger across your hand and the card under it lifts up. Let go to drop it into the formula. Drag down to cancel.

Alternate numbers and operators, up to five cards. Equations are worked out strictly left to right, so the first three cards are bracketed for you: (7 + 3) × 8 = 80.

2

Calculate and hit

Each step is written into the notebook, the result counts up, and the damage lands. The harder the hit compared to the monster's HP, the harder the screen shakes and the louder it sounds.

You only get four calculations per fight. Win early and every calculation or discard you didn't use turns into gold.

3

Pick a reward

After every kill you see your best hit of the fight, where your gold came from, and three ways to upgrade a card:

  • Enhancements: Gold ×2, Fire adds a bonus, Luck has a 25% chance of ×5
  • Operator perks: Amplify (+), Accumulate (+), Reversal (−), Exact (÷)
  • Bigger numbers and transformations that turn a 7 into 7², 2⁷, 7! and beyond
4

Climb the map

Each act is a ten-floor branching map of monsters, elites and shops. Elites hit harder but let you pick two rewards. You'll never be forced into two shops in a row.

Floor ten is the boss. The screen goes dark, its name slams onto a red banner and a heavy bell tolls.

5

Spot exact divisions

An Exact ÷ card multiplies instead of dividing when the numbers divide evenly, then doubles the result.

Place one and every number in your hand that divides evenly glows, so you don't have to do the mental maths: 12 ÷ 4 becomes 12 × 4 × 2 = 96.

6

Swap cards in the shop

Spend gold to swap a shop card into your deck: numbers replace numbers, operators replace operators. Your deck size never changes, so every purchase is a trade-off.

Prices and rewards grow together with the monsters, so gold keeps its value all game long.

7

Ascend a dimension

Every act unlocks a bigger transformation: n², 2ⁿ, eⁿ, n!, ...

After act six the numbers outgrow the page, so the game enters the log stage. Monster HP becomes a power of ten, and damage is the number of digits in your result: 8! × 7⁷ ≈ 33.2 billion deals 10.5 digits. HP and prices restart from the act one curve, and nothing ever overflows.

8

Unlock perks

Reach deeper floors to unlock ten perks, then choose up to three when you start a run. They stay locked in until the run ends.

Each is worth roughly one free shop restock per act, for example a free restock, a reroll of reward choices, or redrawing your whole hand once per act.
